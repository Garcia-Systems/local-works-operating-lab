# Local Works Business Hypothesis

## Central business question

**Can Local Works repeatedly turn business friction into economically sensible, successfully delivered solutions while producing sustainable owner income?**

This is a question, not a claim. Every statement below begins as a hypothesis. No customer result, revenue, or operating history is implied.

## Initial test matrix

| Test | Initial hypothesis | Current evidence status | What would count as evidence | What could falsify it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Demand | Businesses experience meaningful digital workflow friction. | Hypothesis; unproven | Direct observations across qualified businesses, with affected steps, people, and consequences recorded | Repeated audits find only minor inconvenience or no problem customers consider important |
| Value | A Digital Friction Audit can create qualified sales conversations. | Hypothesis; unproven | Prospects complete audits and proceed to discovery around a specific, consequential problem | Audits repeatedly produce no actionable problem or no willingness to continue discovery |
| Sale | Customers will pay Local Works enough for viable margins. | Hypothesis; unproven | Signed proposals, deposits, and measured expected-versus-actual costs showing adequate margin | Qualified customers consistently reject economically honest prices, or delivery costs erase margin |
| Delivery | Delivery partners can implement reliably while Local Works preserves the customer relationship. | Hypothesis; unproven | Partner estimates, milestone performance, acceptance results, quality findings, and customer feedback across engagements | Repeated missed commitments, unacceptable quality, or relationship breakdown despite appropriate selection and leadership |
| Sustainability | Recurring support can contribute to sustainable owner income. | Hypothesis; unproven | Paid renewals plus measured support effort, gross margin, retention, and owner time over a meaningful period | Support demand, churn, pricing, or owner workload repeatedly makes the model economically unsustainable |

## How evidence changes the document

- **Hypothesis** means something currently believed but not yet observed.
- **Observed** means a specific event was witnessed and recorded; one observation does not establish a repeatable pattern.
- **Measured** means a result was quantified with its method and context stated; a measurement still does not automatically prove the broader claim.

Future evidence should identify its source, date, context, and method. Contrary evidence belongs here too. Confidence changes only when the record justifies it.
