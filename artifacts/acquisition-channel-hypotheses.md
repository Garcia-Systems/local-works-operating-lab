# Acquisition Channel Hypotheses

All statements are qualitative, unvalidated hypotheses. Cash burden and owner-time burden are separate; “free” distribution still consumes time. No channel is ranked a definitive winner.

| Channel | May be good for | Cash burden | Owner-time burden | Feedback | Scalability hypothesis | Trust characteristics | Important unknown / evidence required |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Personalized outreach | Direct conversations and market learning | Low | High | Fast if answered | May stay owner-led until a pattern emerges | Relevance must earn attention | Do specific observations earn candid replies? Preserve corrections, conversations, time, and fit. |
| Public Digital Friction Audit | Learning and credibility | Low | High | Moderate | A safe method may repeat; quality may resist automation | Specific evidence helps, but is not diagnosis | Do owners validate impact? Record source, unknowns, discovery outcomes, and effort. |
| Local networking | Relationships, introductions, language learning | Low/moderate | High | Slow; attribution is weak | Relationships may compound but events do not scale directly | Trust can grow through repeated contact | Are relevant owners present? Record useful conversations, introductions, themes, and time. |
| Referral | Trusted introduction and possible direct acquisition | Low cash | High relationship investment | Fast after introduction, slow to establish | Could compound after credible relationships/results exist | Borrows trust; does not prove fit | Will contacts introduce suitable buyers? Record source, fit, and relationship effort. |
| LinkedIn | Targeting, relationships, credibility distribution | Low | Moderate/high | Moderate | Activity may become repeatable, not automatically trusted | Unknown profile has a credibility gap | Are decision makers active and responsive? Measure substantive exchanges, not vanity metrics. |
| Generic outbound email | Direct acquisition or message comparison | Low | Moderate | Potentially fast but shallow | Reach can grow if relevance is learned | Cold unknown sender faces high skepticism | Does a broad message earn relevant conversations? Silence confounds need, message, and delivery. |
| Partnership | Relationships and trusted access | Low/moderate | High to establish | Slow | A few aligned partners might compound | Partner reputation can transfer both ways | Are incentives, customer ownership, and quality aligned? Record qualified introductions and effort. |
| Business/community organization | Local relationships and credibility | Membership/event dependent | High | Slow | Community presence compounds; attribution stays difficult | Participation must be genuine, not extractive | Are target owners involved? Record repeat interactions and relevant introductions. |
| Educational content | Credibility and problem-language learning | Low cash | High | Slow | A useful library might compound if distribution works | Useful judgment can build trust without claims | Do operators consume, reuse, or discuss it? Track qualified questions and owner time. |
| Video | Demonstration and credibility | Low/moderate | High | Slow/moderate | Reusable if topics resonate | Personal presence may help; polish cannot replace evidence | Do relevant viewers raise business questions? Track conversations, not views alone. |
| SEO | Long-term discovery and credibility | Low cash to higher support cost | High/ongoing | Slow | Could compound where genuine search demand exists | Search visibility is not trust by itself | What do qualified buyers search, and with what intent? Require qualified downstream evidence. |
| Paid Facebook/Instagram | Message/audience testing and acquisition | Higher | Moderate | Fast ad feedback; uncertain sales learning | Only plausible after audience, offer, qualification, and economics improve | Cold audience needs a credible offer | Do ads produce qualified conversations, not clicks? Track spend, time, fit, and outcomes. |
| Paid Google/search | Intent capture and direct acquisition | Higher | Moderate | Fast traffic feedback | May scale only with relevant intent and viable economics | Unknown provider must establish confidence quickly | Are terms buyer-relevant? Track intent, qualified conversations, spend, and downstream fit. |

Paid advertising is not inherently bad. Meaningful spend becomes more useful after Local Works understands whom it seeks, what problem resonates, what offer converts, how leads are qualified, approximate customer economics, and customer value. Those facts are presently unknown.
