## Starting hypothesis
State the provisional workflow/problem hypothesis and its audit source. It is not a validated problem.

## Questions

### Current-state questions
- Walk me through what happens today.

### People questions
- Who performs and experiences each step?

### Frequency/volume questions
- How often does it happen? What records could confirm that?

### Time/burden questions
- What does each person do, and has the time been measured?

### Error/exception questions
- What happens when it does not go normally?

### Customer-impact questions
- What delay, confusion, complaint, or abandonment is known?

### Business-impact questions
- What effect on revenue, retention, service, workload, cost, compliance, reliability, or visibility is known?

### System questions
- Which systems support each part, and which capabilities are unknown?

### Policy questions
- Which steps are intentional business choices?

### Constraint questions
- What cannot easily change?

### Urgency questions
- Why consider this now? Is there a real deadline?

### Authority questions
- Who may approve further analysis or later work?

### Budget questions
- Is there plausible willingness and ability to fund improvement? Do not imply approval.

### Success questions
- In workflow and business terms, what would better mean?
