## Website concept review
No implementation is authorized. Valid messages are problem-first discovery, the audit, simplest-sensible solutions, and accountable coordination. Claims of outcomes, customer proof, conversion, savings, or real partners require evidence. Contact and audit flows matter; visual polish and a portal are cosmetic/premature relative to learning. Missing operational needs include decision/change records, cash, capacity, and bounded support.

## Public proof rule
No fake customer logos, testimonials, case studies, percentage improvements, revenue or hours saved, “trusted by” claims, or partner relationships. Harbor Fitness remains fictional. A case study requires a real customer, permission, measured outcome, accurate attribution, and appropriate confidentiality.
