# First Market Experiment

> **SIMULATION — NOT YET RUN IN THE REAL WORLD**

## Market being tested

A small fictional sample of independently operated gyms. Gyms offer recurring, publicly visible membership journeys and may have reachable owner-operators, making them useful for practicing observation and discovery. This is a testable starting hypothesis—not a permanent vertical decision, evidence of demand, or permission to generalize to gyms.

Still unproven: whether meaningful friction exists; whether decision makers care; whether they will respond; whether Local Works is credible; whether any response is economically feasible; and whether any channel produces customers.

## Channels considered

Personalized observation-led outreach, a public Digital Friction Audit, generic outbound email, local networking, referrals, LinkedIn, partnerships, business organizations, content, video, SEO, paid social, and paid search can each support acquisition, credibility, relationships, or learning. None is a proven winner. See `acquisition-channel-hypotheses.md`.

## Experiment 001 — Gym Digital Friction Audit Outreach

**Purpose/question:** Can personalized observations about publicly visible membership friction create useful conversations with independent-gym decision makers?

**Target:** a deliberately small set of fictional independent gyms; no real businesses are contacted.

**Offer:** a brief conversation to correct or validate an observation, not a software pitch.

**Simulated process:**

1. Identify a fictional target and inspect its fictional public customer journey.
2. Record one literal public fact and its source.
3. Separately record a possible friction hypothesis and material unknowns.
4. Draft one accurate, respectful question.
5. Ask for a conversation rather than a sale.
6. Record response or no response without overinterpreting it.
7. Classify the outcome and next step.

**Evidence to collect:** research and outreach owner time; incidental cash/supporting expense; source and observation; message used; role reached; response/no response; correction; validated or rejected friction; authority/constraint information; conversation quality; and permitted next action. Clicks or replies alone are not proof of demand.

## Guardrails

- **Cash:** minimal incidental spending and no advertising buy for this experiment.
- **Time:** precommit a small research/outreach block and stop when it expires.
- Use only legitimately public information; do not scrape or harvest prospects.
- Do not deceive, mystery shop inappropriately, probe security, submit fake transactions, collect unnecessary personal data, or create operational burden.
- Describe facts accurately, keep inference labeled, protect privacy, and never publicly shame a business.
- One respectful follow-up at most in this simulation; honor a refusal immediately.

Total acquisition cost eventually means **cash spending + owner time + supporting expenses**. No detailed CAC or funnel model is claimed here.

## Decision rules

**Continue the hypothesis:** several relevant decision makers independently validate a recurring, meaningful workflow and welcome deeper discovery; the learning value justifies the bounded time/cash cost.

**Change the approach:** observations are frequently corrected, the message is unclear, the wrong role is reached, or silence cannot distinguish delivery from lack of need. Revise one variable and run another bounded experiment rather than scaling.

**Abandon the market hypothesis:** after multiple small, ethically run tests, relevant decision makers consistently report that the suspected workflows are immaterial, already handled, structurally inaccessible, or economically implausible. Record negative evidence rather than manufacturing a project.
