# Agreement topics — educational review checklist

> **THIS IS NOT A CONTRACT. This checklist does not substitute for legal advice or a lawyer-drafted agreement.** It identifies commercial/legal topics that may require professional review; it does not draft jurisdiction-specific terms or claim legal sufficiency.

- [ ] Parties/entities: customer brand versus contracting entity; Local Works brand versus the appropriate contracting entity.
- [ ] Accepted scope and proposal version reference.
- [ ] Price, payment timing, deposit, expenses, and milestones.
- [ ] Observable acceptance criteria and review process.
- [ ] Change evaluation, impact analysis, authorization, price, and timeline concept.
- [ ] Cancellation/termination and treatment of performed work or committed costs.
- [ ] Confidentiality where relevant.
- [ ] Customer data: legitimate authorization, minimum access, test data, revocation, return/deletion.
- [ ] Security/access responsibilities and credential handling.
- [ ] Intellectual property/licensing and custom deliverable ownership.
- [ ] Reusable Local Works materials/methods and customer-specific materials.
- [ ] Third-party software: licenses, pricing, uptime, limitations, support, and policy changes.
- [ ] Subcontractor/delivery-partner use: allowed, notice, approval, and responsibilities.
- [ ] Warranties/defect-correction boundary and excluded new-feature work.
- [ ] Ongoing support boundary and third-party incident responsibility.
- [ ] Limitations and risk allocation where appropriate.
- [ ] Dispute and governing-law topics for qualified counsel.
- [ ] Insurance where relevant.
- [ ] Survival obligations and return/revocation of access and data.

A signed proposal may form part of an agreement in some real situations, but this lab makes no legal conclusion. Its conceptual roles remain separate: a proposal supports a commercial decision; an agreement defines the working relationship.
