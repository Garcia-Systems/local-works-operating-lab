## Evidence quality
**SIMULATION_ONLY.** Passing the simulation means only that the operating model appears coherent under tested assumptions. It does not mean real customers buy, partners quote assumed costs, support matches assumptions, income occurs, or the business is proven.

## Top real-world validation priorities
1. **CRITICAL_NEXT:** Will qualified strangers progress and buy? Method: Run bounded public audits and record funnel progression.
2. **HIGH:** Will customers pay for a bounded solution? Method: Test a proposal without discounting away uncertainty.
3. **HIGH:** Will delivery partners quote and deliver within the model? Method: Request comparable estimates for one bounded scope.
4. **MEDIUM:** How many owner hours does delivery require? Method: Time discovery, coordination, QA, and acceptance.
5. **MEDIUM:** What support and incident tail follows launch? Method: Observe and classify a bounded post-launch period.
6. **MEDIUM:** Will payment timing protect cash? Method: Record invoice-to-cash timing on a real engagement.
