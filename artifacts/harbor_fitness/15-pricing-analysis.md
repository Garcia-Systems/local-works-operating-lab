# Harbor Fitness — Chapter 15 Pricing Analysis

> **This is a fictional training pricing analysis.**
> **This is not a customer quote.**
> Delivery costs remain preliminary unless explicitly validated. Customer value assumptions remain subject to evidence quality. **No proposal has been issued.**

- **Business:** Harbor Fitness
- **Opportunity:** Reduce repeated handling and waiting in membership-freeze requests.
- **Solution direction:** Configure existing tools with bounded automation; validate platform capability before commitment.
- **Scope:** Chapter 14 MUST items plus one SHOULD reporting item; no custom member portal or unrelated workflow.
- **Customer economic context:** The current annual burden is estimated at **$18,600**. Burden is not price and is not automatically recoverable.
- **Recoverable annual value:** **$9,600 hypothesis** before $1,200 annual recurring solution cost.
- **Evidence quality:** Hypothetical/mixed. Operational samples, adoption assumptions, and vendor capability remain to be validated.

## Preliminary Local Works costs

- **Delivery cost:** $2,800 preliminary estimate—not a validated partner quote.
- **Other direct costs:** $400.
- **Owner hours:** 24 hours.
- **Owner-hour internal value:** $75/hour.
- **Imputed owner-time cost:** $1,800. This is an internal planning value, not necessarily customer-visible.
- **Business overhead:** excluded; direct project cost remains separate from portfolio overhead.

## Candidate and two-sided economics

- **Candidate customer price:** $6,000 fixed-fee hypothesis.
- **Local Works contribution:** $2,800 ($6,000 − $2,800 − $400).
- **Contribution margin:** 46.7%.
- **Contribution after owner-time value:** $1,000.
- **Owner-adjusted contribution margin:** 16.7%.
- **Customer annual net benefit:** $8,400 after $1,200 recurring cost.
- **Customer payback:** 8.6 months using the candidate price.
- **Customer 1-year result:** $2,400.
- **Customer 2-year result:** $10,800.
- **Customer 3-year result:** $19,200.

Contribution is not accounting profit: overhead, acquisition cost, taxes, insurance, and other company costs remain.

## Window, sensitivity, and cash

- **Economic price floor:** $5,800 = $2,800 delivery + $400 other direct + $1,800 owner-time value + $800 explicitly selected minimum direct contribution. The $800 is a scenario input, not an industry margin rule.
- **Customer price ceiling / economically sensible range:** $8,400 at Harbor's *stated hypothetical* 12-month payback guardrail; it is not the whole recoverable value and is not an automatic price.
- **Practical pricing window:** $5,800–$8,400, pending evidence. The $6,000 candidate overlaps narrowly with the Local Works floor.
- **Discount sensitivity:** 5% → $5,700 price / $2,500 contribution (10.7% contribution reduction); 10% → $5,400 / $2,200 (21.4% reduction); 20% → $4,800 / $1,600 (42.9% reduction).
- **Payment structure:** 50% ($3,000) deposit at kickoff and 50% at acceptance; this is cash timing, not revenue-recognition guidance.
- **Maximum cash exposure:** $0 in the simplified timing model, compared with $3,200 when all $6,000 is paid on completion. Real dates and payment terms could change this.

## Risks and alternatives

- **Pricing risks:** high platform dependency uncertainty; medium delivery-cost uncertainty; medium customer-value/adoption uncertainty; payment timing; change and support exposure. Mitigate with validation, explicit acceptance, exclusions, guardrails, and refreshed estimates—not a hidden arbitrary buffer.
- **Scope-reduction option:** Remove SHOULD reminder reporting. Illustrative price $5,200, delivery $2,300, other direct $300, owner time 21 hours, and recoverable value $8,700. These inputs change separately; scope reduction does not mechanically equal a discount.
- **Phased option:** Phase 1 paid capability validation at $900; optional Phase 2 bounded implementation at $5,100 only after evidence and a refreshed estimate.
- **No-deal comparison:** If a supported customer ceiling were $4,000 and Local Works' healthy floor $6,000, there would be no pricing window. Simplify, phase, enable internal implementation, leave alone, or decline.
- **Pricing decision:** **PHASE_PROJECT.** Although $6,000 sits inside the present hypothesis window, capability and cost uncertainty make paid validation more responsible than pretending the full engagement is known.
- **Evidence needed before proposal:** Written platform capability evidence; a delivery estimate and range; customer volume/time sample; adoption and realization support; recurring tool price; customer internal effort; acceptance owner; payment timing acceptance; and boundaries for changes and post-acceptance support.
