## Incremental comparison

Integration/automation adds $3,500 implementation cost over configuration while adding only about **-$456 annual net benefit** after its recurring and operating burden. Custom adds $14,000 implementation cost and about **-$2,159 annual net benefit** over configuration. Those increments do not currently justify added ownership and risk, but preliminary inputs could change.

## Configuration sensitivity

| Scenario | Recovery / adoption / realization | Implementation | Annual net benefit | Payback |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Low | 35% / 60% / 65% | $1,300 | $184 | 84.6 months |
| Baseline | 55% / 75% / 80% | $1,000 | $659 | 18.2 months |
| High | 70% / 85% / 90% | $800 | $1,162 | 8.3 months |

Sensitivity is not proof. The **simplest economically sensible alternative** is currently configuration, conditionally; leave alone remains rational if low realization or internal effort is confirmed.

## What evidence could change the result

Observed request counts and handling time; actual steps removed; adoption and exception rates; cash consequences (if any); existing-system capability; new administration; customer internal effort; firm implementation and recurring estimates; and differing useful lives. These are more decision-relevant than unsupported revenue or retention stories.
