# FICTIONAL TRAINING PROPOSAL — Version 1

**Business:** Harbor Fitness
**Opportunity:** Reduce repeated handling of membership-freeze requests

> This is a fictional training proposal. This is not a real customer offer. No legal agreement has been executed. No payment has been collected.

## Executive Summary
Harbor Fitness currently handles routine membership-freeze requests through repeated staff intervention. Local Works recommends a paid capability-validation phase, followed only if supported by the simplest configuration or integration path. This engagement is limited to the agreed freeze workflow and does not replace the membership platform.

## Current Situation
Members submit requests that front-desk and management staff repeatedly review, clarify, decide, record, and confirm. The frequency and $18,600 annual burden used in the pricing exercise are **hypothetical**; direct cash savings and retention effects remain **UNKNOWN**. Platform capability is an important constraint.

## Validated Problem
Eligible freeze requests cannot currently be completed without repeated staff handling, creating administrative work and follow-up.

## Why It Matters
Based on current assumptions, routine handling consumes capacity and creates delay and inconvenience. A modeled $9,600 recoverable annual value is hypothetical, not promised ROI.

## Recommended Approach
**Paid validation phase**, then CONFIGURE if supported; INTEGRATE only if required and separately approved.

## Why This Approach
Configuration may satisfy the bounded workflow with less complexity and ownership burden. A custom portal is not recommended while existing-platform capability is unvalidated.

## Business Outcome
Reduce repeated handling of eligible freeze requests while preserving eligibility rules and manager approval for exceptions.

## Included Scope
- Request capture and required data
- Eligibility determination using approved rules
- Manager routing for exceptions
- Decision recording and confirmation
- Trigger: member submits a freeze request for a defined membership type
- End: decision is recorded and confirmation is sent

## Excluded Scope
- Membership cancellation, disputes, refunds, upgrades/downgrades
- Full account management, mobile application, platform replacement

## Customer Responsibilities
Provide approved rules, decision maker, least-privilege test access, sanitized test data, timely review/testing, approvals, and approved third-party fees.

## Local Works Responsibilities
Requirements translation, coordination, solution oversight, customer communication, QA and acceptance coordination, and documentation coordination.

## Expected Delivery Responsibilities
Technical implementation will use an approved delivery path selected after acceptance and technical validation. That team will perform validated implementation and support testing/documentation; no delivery company is currently selected.

## Assumptions
The current vendor supports required configuration or integration; Harbor can provide test access; documented policy is accurate; no major migration occurs. If a critical assumption is invalidated, scope, cost, or timing may require revision.

## Dependencies
Customer decision-maker availability, approved rules, vendor capability, test access, and an approved delivery path.

## Acceptance Criteria
Eligible requests capture required data and record eligibility; exceptions route to an authorized manager; decisions are recorded and the agreed confirmation is produced.

## Business Success Metrics
Post-delivery handling time and share of routine requests completed without repeated handling. These are measurements, not guaranteed acceptance outcomes.

## Investment

## Recurring / Third-Party Costs

## Risks / Constraints
Vendor capability, policy completeness, delivery estimate, adoption, and third-party changes remain constraints.

## Optional Future Work
