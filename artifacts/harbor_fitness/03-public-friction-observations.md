# Harbor Fitness: Public Friction Observations

> **Entirely fictional.** These are invented public-facing pages for a learning exercise, not internal facts, a diagnosis, or evidence about any real business.

## Observation 1 — membership changes

- **Source/context:** fictional Harbor Fitness public membership FAQ.
- **Observed fact:** “Contact the front desk for membership freezes or cancellations.”
- **Possible friction:** telephone handling *may* consume staff time or inconvenience some members.
- **Unknowns:** request frequency, call duration, customer sentiment, staffing, legal/business reasons, and whether existing software supports an online path.
- **What Local Works must NOT assume:** that calls are frequent, costly, disliked, unnecessary, or technically fixable; that an online cancellation flow is appropriate; or that Harbor Fitness wants a change.
- **Potential audit/discovery question:** “How are freeze and cancellation requests currently handled, and does that process create meaningful work or member complaints?”

## Observation 2 — class registration

- **Source/context:** fictional public class page.
- **Observed fact:** the page lists a weekly timetable and tells visitors to call to confirm first-visit availability.
- **Possible friction:** callers and staff *may* need extra coordination before a first class.
- **Unknowns:** call volume, capacity volatility, walk-in policy, no-show controls, current booking-system capabilities, and whether personal contact improves conversion or safety.
- **What Local Works must NOT assume:** that the process is manual internally, that a listed class has open capacity, or that removing the call would improve the experience.
- **Potential audit/discovery question:** “What purpose does the confirmation call serve, and does the current process create any work or lost visits worth addressing?”

## Observation 3 — staffed hours

- **Source/context:** fictional public contact page.
- **Observed fact:** general opening hours and shorter staffed front-desk hours are displayed separately.
- **Possible friction:** visitors may be uncertain when staff-dependent requests can be completed.
- **Unknowns:** whether visitors misunderstand the distinction, which tasks need staff, how questions are handled after hours, and whether complaints occur.
- **What Local Works must NOT assume:** that customers are confused, messages go unanswered, or staffing should change.
- **Potential audit/discovery question:** “Do members ever have difficulty distinguishing access hours from the hours when staff-dependent requests are available?”

## Reasoning rule

A public observation earns a question, not a verdict. Local Works must accurately cite the public context, label the possible friction as inference, seek correction privately and respectfully, and validate frequency, impact, constraints, and authority before calling anything a business problem.
