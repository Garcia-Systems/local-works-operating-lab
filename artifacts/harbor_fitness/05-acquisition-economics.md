## What is and is not learned

The case illustrates that research and qualification consume investment before a sale and that investment remains part of cumulative channel economics if the prospect never buys. It does not show that the friction is validated, Harbor Fitness will buy, the channel converts, or any acquisition cost is viable. A future sale would permit a cohort/period CAC calculation only when all failed attempts and attribution choices are also included.
