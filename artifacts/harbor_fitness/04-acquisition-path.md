# Harbor Fitness — simulated acquisition path

> Harbor Fitness is fictional. Every event below is a simulation for learning, not a Local Works contact or result. **SIMULATED OUTPUT IS NOT OBSERVED EVIDENCE.**

## Step 1 — public-friction research

**Simulated event:** Public-friction research.

**What happened:** Local Works reviews the invented public membership journey recorded in Chapter 3 and writes a narrow observation without diagnosing a business problem.

**Evidence gained:** Within the simulation, only a public-facing workflow observation and possible interpretations exist. No customer pain is validated.

**Still unknown:** Internal process, frequency, business impact, authority, budget, urgency, technical constraints, and whether the observation is even material remain unknown—not bad.

**Next decision:** Decide whether a respectful, bounded outreach message is justified.

## Step 2 — personalized outreach

**Simulated event:** A personalized message is sent.

**What happened:** The message states the observation, labels the interpretation as tentative, and asks whether it is accurate; it does not pitch a predetermined portal.

**Evidence gained:** The simulation tests an observation-led message shape. Sending is activity, not response or business progress.

**Still unknown:** Whether any real decision maker would read, answer, or value it, and whether the contact has authority.

**Next decision:** Wait within the experiment's follow-up limit; treat silence as ambiguous.

## Step 3 — response

**Simulated event:** Harbor Fitness replies.

**What happened:** A fictional operator says the observation is broadly recognizable but cannot quantify its frequency or cost and is willing to answer a few audit questions.

**Evidence gained:** In this simulated narrative, the observation is not immediately rejected and earns limited engagement. It does not establish real demand, economics, or a sale.

**Still unknown:** Root cause, magnitude, customer impact, priority, decision process, budget, and feasible response.

**Next decision:** Invite a bounded Digital Friction Audit and preserve corrections and uncertainty.

## Step 4 — Digital Friction Audit / possible qualification

**Simulated event:** Enough fictional audit information is supplied for review.

**What happened:** The answers suggest a potentially relevant workflow and substantial unresolved unknowns. Local Works marks Harbor Fitness “possible qualification,” not “customer.”

**Evidence gained:** Only the simulation's stated workflow detail and willingness to continue. No internal economics appear by magic, and no proposal is appropriate yet.

**Still unknown:** Verified burden, economic value, authority, constraints, urgency, solution choice, willingness to pay, and profitable deliverability.

**Next decision:** Decide whether the evidence justifies scarce discovery time. Stop here before discovery; qualification may protect capacity.
