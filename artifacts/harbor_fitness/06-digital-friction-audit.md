# Harbor Fitness — Digital Friction Audit

> **FICTIONAL TRAINING SCENARIO — NOT A REAL LOCAL WORKS CUSTOMER**

**Business context:** Harbor Fitness is a fictional local gym used to teach the
audit method. No claim here describes an actual organization.  
**Audit scope:** The public customer journey plus two explicitly fictional internal
observations: a staff system demonstration and manager context.  
**Evidence sources:** Fictional public website, membership flow and document;
fictional direct observation, employee statement, manager statement, and system
demonstration. No measured operational dataset was available.

## Customer journey

The relevant journey is **Find → Understand → Contact → Join → Pay → Use/Receive

audit would omit irrelevant stages.

## Findings, knowledge, and questions

### A. Observed friction worth investigating

**Manual join-form re-entry.** Known: customers complete the online path, and the
fictional demonstration corroborates staff copying data into a disconnected system.
Frequency was described as frequent, but weekly volume, handling time, errors,
system constraints, and business impact remain unknown. Ask: *How often is data
copied, how long does it take, and what errors or exceptions occur?*

**Routine membership changes.** Known: public instructions require front-desk
contact. The customer-friction and employee-burden claims remain hypotheses.
Unknowns include request frequency, handling time, satisfaction, billing effects,
system capability, and whether a policy or legal reason applies. The fictional
business objective is a helpful retention conversation; the tradeoff is member
control versus that conversation. Ask: *Why is contact required, and what happens
from request through billing?*

### B. Possible friction requiring more evidence

The membership comparison omits some terms. It may create repeat questions, but no
question volume, abandonment, or employee effort has been observed. Review the full
join flow and ask staff which explanations recur.

### C. Low-significance friction / false positive

The call required for legal-name correction first looked like avoidable friction.
Additional fictional manager evidence says it is extremely rare and identity
verification is required. Unless complaints or errors emerge, probably leave it
alone. Visible inconvenience is not necessarily a worthwhile opportunity.

### D. No meaningful friction / working adequately

Location discovery, optional tours, the observed payment path, staffed card check-in,
and return visits appear adequate. The tour and greeting illustrate that human
interaction is not automatically bad. These conclusions are bounded to observed
paths; they do not prove the absence of exceptions.

### E. Unknown internal workflow areas

Payment failure and reconciliation, peak check-in exceptions, billing changes,
request volumes, handoff status, error rates, and the capabilities of existing
systems cannot be established publicly. The hidden join-form re-entry demonstrates
why internal discovery matters even when the customer-facing flow looks polished.

## Audit recommendation

**DISCOVERY RECOMMENDED.** A focused discovery is justified because internal
evidence corroborates manual join-data re-entry, while the membership-management
path may affect three parties and needs policy and workload context. Discovery
should measure frequency, handling time and errors; map the employee handoffs; and
clarify policy, billing, and system constraints. Multiple other stages work
adequately, so the evidence does not support a broad transformation.

**Discovery recommended does not mean project recommended.** Discovery may conclude
that a simple process change, configuration, or leaving the workflow alone is best.

## Explicit limitations

- This is fictional training material, not a Local Works result or customer claim.
- Public evidence cannot reveal most internal effort, exception handling, sentiment,
  root causes, contractual obligations, or system capability.
- Frequencies and severities remain `UNKNOWN` unless explicitly supported above;
  missing information has not been treated as low.
- No financial impact has yet been established.
- No technical solution has yet been selected.
- No custom software recommendation has been made.
