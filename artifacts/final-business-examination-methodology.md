# Final Business Examination Methodology

## Verdict method
The final gate consumes the Chapter 32A `BusinessSimulationResult` and Chapter 32B owner-income, scenario, sensitivity, Monte Carlo, bottleneck, and operating-model objects. It does not rerun arithmetic by hand or use a hidden composite. Demand, qualification, sales, customer value, project economics, delivery, quality, support, incidents, expansion, retention, referrals, cash, customer and partner concentration, vendor dependency, owner capacity and income, stability, repeatability, continuity, and evidence quality remain visible dimensions. Multiple qualifiers may coexist.

## Evidence quality and simulation versus reality
`SIMULATION_ONLY` remains the current level because no genuine operations are present. Repeated calculation cannot upgrade evidence. Passing means the operating model appears coherent under tested assumptions; it does not establish buying, partner quotes, support burden, income, or proof. Failure is useful: trace the failing scenario to an assumption, lifecycle stage, bottleneck, and lever before spending real cash.

## Owner-income quality
Amount is reviewed with monthly stability, total and after-hours workload, cash backing, customer concentration, and partner dependency. Revenue and contribution are not owner income. A high Year 3 draw alone cannot authorize full-time operation.

## Conditions, bottlenecks, and uncertainty
Success and failure conditions are tied to prior scenario evidence. The baseline primary bottleneck and 32B period evolution are reused. Ranked sensitivities determine which uncertain assumptions deserve early validation. Monte Carlo values are simulation frequencies under configured distributions, never real-world probabilities.

## Operating mode
Project-only, pay-as-you-go, light support, managed support, and mixed models are compared alongside side-, part-, and full-time capacity. Side business is a legitimate verdict. Full-time requires cash, pipeline, support, workload, risk, and income stability—not revenue alone.

## Evidence gaps and validation priority
Priority combines sensitivity, uncertainty, cost of error, and ease of a bounded test. Experiments state scope, time, cash, evidence, success/failure signals, and the next decision. The first goal is replacing a high-sensitivity funnel assumption with real evidence, not building a platform.

## Production-software gate and dogfooding
Software readiness is independent from business viability. Local Works applies **Configure → Integrate → Automate → Custom Build → Leave Alone** to itself, asking whether existing tools are adequate before code. Capability records preserve observed need, workaround, evidence, frequency, pain, approach, priority, and unknowns.

## First-real-customer minimum
A contact path, audit and discovery artifacts, proposal, existing project tracking, shared files, decision/change logs, calendar, and support contact can manage one bounded customer manually. Manual operation is a validation strategy, not failure.

## Public site versus portal
A small truthful site may explain Home, How It Works, Digital Friction Audit, Capabilities, and Contact before a customer portal is justified. It must contain no fabricated proof. Portal, custom back office, and automation wait for repeated observed need.

## Final interpretation
The lab shows a coherent, testable operating hypothesis and identifies risks. It does not prove a business. The next action must produce evidence, and production software must earn its gate through actual operating need.
