# Opportunity Gate Methodology

## 1. Purpose

The gate asks whether an audit finding is worth additional Local Works investigation.
It prevents the invalid leap **friction exists → project exists**. Its sequence is:
**Audit Finding → Opportunity Assessment → Decision**.

## 2. Inputs from the Digital Friction Audit

Use the observed fact, distinct friction hypothesis, evidence source and status,
affected parties, frequency, severity, workaround, disposition, known facts, unknowns,
and follow-up questions. Do not upgrade public inference into internal evidence.

## 3. Opportunity dimensions

Assess frequency, impact, affected parties, workaround burden, business importance,
urgency, authority, measurability, technical plausibility, economic plausibility, and
evidence strength. Economic plausibility asks only *could this be worth measuring?*
It is not quantified economics, ROI, or a budget assumption.

Record inspectable statements rather than a magic score. Show positive signals,
negative signals, unknowns, disqualifiers, the decision, and its reason. Apparent
precision such as `73/100 = qualified` hides the judgment the gate exists to expose.

## 4. Problem potential versus commercial fit

**Problem potential** concerns significance: parties affected, burden, importance,
and a plausible improvement path. **Commercial fit** concerns whether Local Works is
appropriately positioned to investigate: urgency, sponsor authority, access, and
capability. A strong problem can have weak access; easy owner access can accompany a
negligible problem. Preserve both conclusions instead of averaging them.

## 5. Unknown handling

`UNKNOWN ≠ BAD`: unknown frequency is not rare, unknown budget is not no budget,
and unknown feasibility is not impossible. Name each unknown and the question that
could resolve it. Critical missing evidence can produce `MORE_INFORMATION_NEEDED`
without becoming a negative signal.

## 6. Hard disqualifiers

Record conditions that make pursuit inappropriate: unethical outcomes, unsafe
expectations, demanded misrepresentation, refusal to investigate a predetermined
risky answer, no viable authority path, or work clearly beyond Local Works's
capability. Preserve the reason. Problem severity does not cancel a disqualifier.

## 7. Grouping related findings

Multiple findings may be manifestations of one business workflow. Group them only
when an explicit rationale connects their process, actors, handoffs, or shared
condition. Ask: *independent annoyances, or symptoms of the same process?* Do not
automatically group findings merely because they came from one audit.

## 8. Symptom versus workflow opportunity

Frame the candidate around a business condition, not a complaint. “Customers call
constantly” may point to routine membership changes requiring staff intervention.
“Lots of emails” may point to customers lacking appointment status. “Employees hate
the system” may point to triple data entry. The complaint is a clue, not yet the
opportunity definition.

## 9. Decision categories

- `DISCOVERY_WARRANTED`: evidence and fit justify focused learning.
- `MORE_INFORMATION_NEEDED`: critical gate evidence is missing.
- `SIMPLE_IMPROVEMENT`: an obvious, bounded, low-risk correction can bypass discovery.
- `MONITOR`: retain the signal and watch for material change.
- `LEAVE_ALONE`: friction is real but intervention is not justified.
- `REFER_ELSEWHERE`: the valid need belongs with a better-suited professional.
- `DISQUALIFY`: engagement conditions make pursuit inappropriate.

## 10. When discovery is warranted

Discovery is proportionate when the workflow condition has credible problem
potential, commercial access is sufficient, critical evidence is not wholly absent,
professional investigation could add value, and no simpler exit or disqualifier
controls. It authorizes questions and measurement—not delivery.

## 11. When Local Works should stop

Stop when a simple correction resolves the issue, the established burden is
negligible, monitoring is sufficient, a specialist should lead, or a hard
disqualifier exists. Referral and walking away are responsible outcomes, not lost
scores.

## 12. Qualification is not project approval

The gate does not prove economics, approve implementation, select configure versus
integrate versus automate versus build, set a price, or promise results.
`DISCOVERY_WARRANTED` means only: **the problem appears worth investigating**.
