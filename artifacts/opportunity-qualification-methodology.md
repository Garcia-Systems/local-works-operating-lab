# Opportunity qualification methodology

## Why this gate exists
Qualification asks whether an opportunity deserves more scarce Local Works owner time. A real problem plus a real economic burden can still be a poor deal when the customer does not prioritize it, authority is absent, change is infeasible, fit is weak, or risk is unacceptable. A justified stop is a successful outcome.

## Qualification is not discovery
Discovery asks what is happening; reconstruction explains how work happens; economics estimates current burden. Qualification consumes that evidence and asks whether to continue. It does not repeat interviews wholesale, select technology, calculate recoverable value/ROI, approve a project, or create a proposal.

## Independent dimensions
- **Problem quality:** Is there an evidence-backed business workflow, affected parties, exceptions, policy, constraints, and no critical unresolved contradiction? “We need AI” alone is weak.
- **Economic significance:** Carry Chapter 10's state forward. `ECONOMICALLY_TRIVIAL` favors decline; `MORE_EVIDENCE_REQUIRED` remains a gap; `POTENTIALLY_MEANINGFUL` differs from measured `MEANINGFUL_BURDEN_ESTABLISHED`. Never invent intangible value to rescue a deal.
- **Customer priority:** Local Works believing the burden matters is not evidence that management intends to act. Low priority normally means nurture or stop for now.
- **Urgency:** Record an evidenced “why now,” such as growth, renewal, deadline, expansion, workload, complaints, or an initiative. “No particular reason” lowers sales priority but is not automatically fatal.
- **Authority:** Identify reachable roles as context requires: user, influencer, champion, decision maker, budget owner, technical approver, and procurement/other approver. One owner may hold several roles; a corporate manager may hold none of the needed authority.
- **Budget/capacity:** Distinguish `KNOWN_BUDGET`, `PLAUSIBLE_CAPACITY`, `UNKNOWN`, `INSUFFICIENT`, and `UNWILLING_TO_INVEST`. Unknown is not no. Ask about existing systems spend, willingness if value is established, budget cycle, control, approval level, and competition with other investments—without guessing a price.
- **Technical plausibility:** Ask only whether at least one improvement path might exist and what vendor/API/access uncertainty remains. Possible does not mean economically sensible.
- **Organizational feasibility:** Ask whether contracts, corporate/franchise control, policy ownership, regulation, access, and adoption permit the workflow to change.
- **Measurability:** Ask whether success could eventually be observed with a defensible before/after measure. Difficult measurement can be a concern without being a disqualifier.
- **Local Works fit:** Favor business-workflow friction needing business/technical translation and responsibly coordinated delivery. Branding, accounting, legal analysis, emergency incident response, commodity break/fix, oversized transformation, or unsupported specialties favor referral.
- **Commercial risk:** Contextually classify rushed deadlines, refusal to answer, contingent payment, unlimited-change demands, unclear approval, and unrealistic comparisons as `CONCERN`, `MAJOR_CONCERN`, or `DISQUALIFIER`; do not become cynical.

## Hard disqualifiers
Illegal/unethical aims, deception, unsafe expectations, unacceptable access, abuse, no authorized path, irresponsible guarantees, or work far outside capability with no responsible delivery path cannot be averaged away by strong signals. A hard disqualifier produces `DISQUALIFY`.

## Owner-time protection
Estimate remaining discovery, vendor research, technical investigation, proposal preparation, and partner sourcing. A marginal lead needing 20 unpaid hours competes with a clearer lead needing three. This introduces opportunity cost without pretending to calculate portfolio capacity.

## Transparent decisions
- `ADVANCE_TO_SOLUTION_DESIGN`: evidence supports another bounded stage; unresolved details can still be tested there.
- `MORE_EVIDENCE_REQUIRED`: a decision-critical, answerable gap blocks responsible advancement.
- `NURTURE`: credible opportunity, but customer priority/timing does not support present pursuit.
- `REFER_ELSEWHERE`: legitimate need with weak Local Works fit and a responsible outside path.
- `DECLINE`: pursuit is unattractive, such as trivial burden or insufficient/unwilling capacity.
- `DISQUALIFY`: a hard ethical, authorization, safety, conduct, or delivery boundary applies.

There is no magic score. Record positive signals, concerns, unknowns, hard disqualifiers, decision, rationale, next action, and expected effort. Qualification is permission to invest the next bounded increment—not project approval, a promise to propose, or a guaranteed sale.
