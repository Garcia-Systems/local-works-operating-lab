# Relationship expansion methodology

Retention is a customer's voluntary choice to continue because value, trust, and economics remain sensible—not operational **lock-in** through withheld credentials, knowledge, or vendor access. Relationship health is a qualitative, evidence-backed view of customer value, reliability, support, trust, communication, commercial health, owner burden, dependencies, outcomes, engagement, and future opportunity. It is not a magic score: health and profitability are independent, and a quiet customer may be healthy.

## Review value before work

Use a cadence proportional to the relationship: monthly only where warranted, otherwise quarterly, semiannual, on-demand, or none. Review what works, remaining friction, support and incidents, manual work, feedback, changed systems and priorities, and measured outcomes. Preserve measured, partially measured, observed, anecdotal, expected-only, and unknown evidence. Expected benefit is not realized ROI; customer value is distinct from value the relationship creates for Local Works.

A repeated support question, deferred change, workaround, new process/location, limitation, request, staff burden, disconnected workflow, or measured bottleneck can be an **expansion signal**. A casual idea, one employee's preference, spare budget, owner enthusiasm, desired revenue, or apparent reuse is not enough. Support activity alone is not expansion. Repetition may justify linkage from support to a signal, never an automatic proposal.

## Qualify, then decide

Run existing-customer work through the same problem, frequency, burden, priority, authority, capacity/budget, fit, urgency, measurability, feasibility, simpler-alternative, discovery, scope, pricing, and economic gates as new work. The path remains signal → qualification → discovery → economics → alternatives → scope → price → proposal → decision. Trust does not repair a bad deal.

Compare current burden and recoverable value with implementation cost, customer price/payback, delivery cost, contribution, owner effort, support impact, and new risk. Relationship review, discovery, and proposal time mean expansion acquisition is not free. Contribution per expansion owner hour makes that effort visible. Never assume an incumbent discount.

**Leave alone** is legitimate when burden/frequency are low, a workaround is acceptable, customization is risky or expensive, or a vendor may solve it. “Probably not worth changing right now” can preserve more trust than a sale. Conversely, calling meaningful repeated friction “support” forever withholds value. Handle true support in support; separately qualify changed workflow.

## Retention, churn, and transition

Retention may be support, repeat projects, occasional pay-as-you-go help, periodic review, or no active work with a positive relationship. Small cohorts and counts need context; do not manufacture vanity renewal, expansion, or referral percentages. Churn means an expected ongoing relationship intentionally ends, but project-complete/no-support-needed churn can be healthy. Poor support, defects, billing surprises, overselling, communication failure, and dependency-driven loss are unhealthy. Record observable risk signals—complaints, incidents, disputes, value evidence, partner/vendor change, payment, or expectations—without inferring emotion.

Actions include no action, check-in, value review, documentation, support-plan adjustment, issue resolution, discovery, vendor referral, and graceful offboarding. Offboarding conceptually covers documentation, access, issues, ownership, contacts, commercial status, and termination timing. It is not implemented here.

## Referrals, proof, and reuse

Ask only after stable delivery and supported value, with no major dispute and appropriate timing. A request is optional, specific to a relevant workflow/business/association, easy to decline, and never payment owed for service. Track referral leads, qualification, discovery, sales, and acquisition effort; referrals are not free or automatic. The lab models only unsent requests and simulated pipeline states.

Public case studies, testimonials, metrics, quotes, and logo use require a real customer, accurate measured evidence, explicit permission, and confidentiality review. Satisfaction never implies permission. Internal learning may record the problem, path, economics, burden, lessons, and reusable patterns without claiming public proof. One project supports at most a hypothesis or early signal; repeated observations are required for repeated-pattern or strong-evidence confidence.

Cumulative observed relationship economics preserve—not overwrite—project, support, incident, expansion, goodwill, and other direct records. Track acquisition, delivery, support, incident, review, expansion, and referral/admin owner hours, plus monthly relationship overhead for memory and readiness. Use observed or simulated cumulative contribution; any lifetime projection must be explicitly a scenario. Expansion and referral pipelines separate ideas/readiness from qualified, proposed, won, or received outcomes. Broader portfolio scaling belongs to Chapter 31.
